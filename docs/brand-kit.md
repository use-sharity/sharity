## 3. Brand Voice

**Tone:** Friendly and plain-spoken. Sounds like a real person, not a startup. Calm, direct, warm. Says what it means in as few words as possible.

**Always:** Practical, specific, grounded in everyday situations.

**Never:** Word-buzzy, moralistic.

### Do / Don't

| Say this | Not this |
|----------|----------|
| Borrow from neighbors you already trust | Reclaim community through shared resources |
| No need to buy something you'll use once | Divest from material possessions |
| Someone nearby probably has it | Empower your local ecosystem |
| Share it. Borrow it. Keep it simple. | Sustainable peer-to-peer exchange |
| Here for half a year? Borrow instead of buy. | Reduce your consumption footprint |
